Saksham is a computational biologist in the Reznik Laboratory. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Economics at Cornell University, followed by a Master’s degree from Cornell Tech. Before joining the lab, he worked as a software engineer at Amazon Web Services and PMG Digital Agency. In the Reznik Lab, he works on understanding treatment-related toxicities in cancer patients, using large language models and multimodal clinical data to detect, grade, and characterize adverse events at scale. He is broadly interested in how patient-level clinical and molecular features shape who experiences toxicity, and in building tools that help clinicians anticipate and manage these side effects.
