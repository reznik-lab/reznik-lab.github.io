Rachel is a postdoctoral research scholar in the Reznik Laboratory. She earned her PhD in Molecular and Systems Biology at Dartmouth College, where she developed a CRISPR-based dynamic lineage tracing system to study the evolution of chemotherapy resistance in leukemia. In the Reznik Lab, she studies the role of mitochondrial DNA mutations in cancer. She is broadly interested in using multiomic technologies to better understand tumor evolution and predict treatment response.

Outside of the lab, Rachel enjoys drawing, playing pickleball, and wandering aimlessly around Roosevelt Island.
